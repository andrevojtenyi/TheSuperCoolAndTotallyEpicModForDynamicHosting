Privacy policy

Minecraft Shared Worlds is a Minecraft mod that runs on your own computer. This policy describes how it uses your Google account.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, the mod asks only for the "Google Drive: files created or opened by this app" permission (drive.file). With it, the mod can see and change only the folders and files it creates itself, for the Minecraft worlds you choose to share. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

What it stores, and where

  • Your shared Minecraft world files are stored in a folder in your own Google Drive. They are encrypted with a key that is protected by a passcode you choose.
  • The sign-in token Google gives the app is stored on your own computer, and inside the invite code you choose to give to people you want to play with. Anyone you give an invite code to can use it to reach those world files, so share it only with people you trust.

What it does not do

  • The developer does not receive, collect, store or have access to your Google data, your tokens or your worlds. The mod talks directly between your computer and Google.
  • Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics.

Removing access

You can revoke the mod's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its folder from your Google Drive.
